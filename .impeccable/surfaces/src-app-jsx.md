# Surface brief: the portfolio page (src/App.jsx and src/components/site/*)

Scope: the whole single-page site. Visitor mode: **Persuade** (success is an inbound message; no showable artifact leads, so the person and his competence must persuade).

Audience and job: hiring managers and recruiters for GCP DevOps / SRE roles deciding within a minute whether to write; engineering peers reading deeper. Action: contact form, email, LinkedIn, resume download. Proof: the resume facts, the Credly badge, public repos (SpaceWarden, BharatLeafLens, PDF-Signer, MemeFetchingRedditAPI). Constraints: Amit leads the first viewport; no metrics slab (figures may live inside narrative only); TCS / Deutsche Börse / C7-SCS named; no code links on client work; meme section stays; Web3Forms contact with sending/sent/failed; reduced-motion static fallback; facts never hidden by style.

Owner decisions this round (2026-09-23): a complete cinematic redesign. "Cinematic" means the **quality** of cinema (scale, controlled light, depth, pacing) in any world, not literal film furniture; the owner declined the literal main-title reading. Dark-only still holds as **one scheme, no toggle, `color-scheme: dark`** — but the page is now a dark hall containing genuinely lit surfaces: large luminous plates carrying dark ink, never light text floating on near-black. The retired Mission Control vocabulary is discarded wholesale, including its invented callsigns (FIDO, BOOSTER, GNC, EECOM, INCO) and section names (CONSOLES, MISSION LOG, FLIGHT RECORDS, DOWNLINK, SYSTEMS BOARD, CAPCOM), none of which appear in PRODUCT.md's terminology-to-preserve list.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Throw. A dark hall with one projector in it, where everything you read is what is lit and rank is carried by light and depth of field rather than by a border. Amit stands where the beam meets him. It refuses the console room it replaces — every panel, seam, well, lamp and callsign of it — and it refuses the letterboxed title-card page that "cinematic" produces by default.

OWN-WORLD: The hall is an unlit warm charcoal (#17130E family), never pure black and never an emissive void. The gate is a luminous bone plate (#EFE7D6) carrying dark warm ink (#1B1712) — the testable discriminator for this world is that body copy is **dark ink on a lit ground**, not light text on a dark ground behind a radial gradient. One tungsten lamp (#FFD49C) is the page's only light source: it tints everything inside the gate, halates at the plate's edges, and desaturates everything outside it; no second hue is introduced anywhere for decoration. One leader red (#D2412C) is the single signal, for faults and the live marker. Falloff is real and computed from the lamp position, not a decorative vignette, and dust drifts in the beam. Enclosure is gone: no panels, no cards, no seams, no dividers, no wells. Structure is light: a lit plate, a half-lit margin, an unlit hall. Type: a heavy condensed grotesk at title scale for ink in the light, a plain humanist face for prose, and a small tracked mono for slate data — no boxes around any of it, hairline rules only.

STORY: The visitor arrives in a dark hall and finds one thing lit. In the light is a man and his name, and under it the platform he keeps up: a regulated securities-clearing system on Google Cloud, and the Google certification for it. As they scroll the beam re-frames and they read scene by scene — what he operates, where he has worked, what he has built, what he knows — each one lit, each one specific, none of it claimed with a superlative. They act by stepping into the light: the contact form, email, LinkedIn, or the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full height. The hall is unlit warm charcoal. A single luminous bone gate owns roughly two thirds of the frame, offset to the right of centre on the lamp's throw axis, its light falling off toward the corners with dust drifting through it. Amit's portrait sits where the beam lands, graded so the lit half of him matches the plate and the shadow half dissolves into the hall. His name is set inside the light at plate scale in dark ink, overlapping the portrait's edge the way title ink sits on a frame. Beneath it, the role and "Google Cloud Certified Professional Cloud DevOps Engineer" as slate data, then the lede naming Deutsche Börse Group's C7-SCS via TCS. A left rail in the unlit hall carries small readouts on hairline rules — location, the live IST/CET time, the certification — with no boxes. The primary action, "Step into the light" (scrolls to the first scene), sits in the lit plate as dark ink on tungsten; "Download resume" sits beside it as a hairline-ruled link. Along the bottom edge, one unbroken horizontal strip of scene numbers that scales rather than wraps, marking where NOW is.

FORM: The Throw, candidate 6 on my ordered list, dealt by the roll and locked by the owner on the decision page. Seed key 01eb5fa9. Code-led: no image generation exists on this machine, so the ambition lives in this contract, in the FIRST VIEWPORT block and in the signature interaction below, audited in behavior at finish. Raises carried in from the challengers it beat: one light source governing every colour (indoor weather sun); enclosure never, rank by depth alone (cracktro scroller); the light field commits at page scale, 60%+ of the frame (seedbed lobes); layout registers to the beam's geometry, not a neutral centred container (curved-crease shell); one unbroken element that scales instead of wrapping and always says where NOW is (drum machine step row).

Signature interaction and motion grammar: **THE GATE** — one luminous rectangle that is the page's only light source and that re-frames per scene as you scroll: its aspect, position and throw change (wide for the hero, taller for the records), the lamp flares briefly at each change, and the incoming plate resolves from slightly soft to sharp like a focus pull. Secondary motion: dust drifting in the beam on a single shared clock; a barely perceptible gate weave; the live IST/CET readout ticking; hover anywhere is a change in how much light falls on a thing, never a lift and never a border. **Content is visible by default and motion is enhancement only** — nothing waits for a dissolve to become readable, because the visitor is scanning in sixty seconds. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every scene renders fully lit and static, the dust and the weave stop, and the clock still ticks.

Scene plan after the first viewport, in the new world's own vocabulary (the retired callsigns and section names are gone): WHAT HE RUNS (the five disciplines, renamed from their invented callsigns to their real domains, one competence sentence and the real tools each, no numbers); WHERE HE HAS WORKED (experience, figures kept inside the lines); WHAT HE HAS BUILT (six records: four client items plus PDF Digital Signer API and Bharat Leaf Lens, GitHub links only on the public repos, client work never linked); THE FEED (the r/ProgrammerHumor live meme with its refresh and failure state, lit as its own plate); WHAT HE KNOWS (skills); CERTIFICATION and EDUCATION; and the close, the contact form and direct channels in the fullest light on the page. Footer in the unlit hall.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
